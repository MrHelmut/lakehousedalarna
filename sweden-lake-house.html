SUMMER IN DALARNA

Lake house in Sweden with private beach and sauna

Lake House Dalarna, also known as Solsidan Dalarna, is a private holiday home directly beside Lake Rogsjön in central Sweden. With around 200 m², three bedrooms and room for up to six guests, it is a place for families and small groups to slow down together.

A Swedish summer at your own pace

The private sandy beach is approximately 25 metres from the house. Start with a swim, take breakfast on the terrace and leave the afternoon open for a boat trip or time in the forest. A boat and SUP are available for days on the lake; the private sauna and relaxation area offer a warm ending to the day.

Your own sandy beach

Swim, sit by the water or enjoy the lake view from the house.

Sauna evenings

A private place to unwind after a day outdoors.

Time on the lake

Boat and life jackets are included. Guests pay for petrol and refill it before departure.

Space for the whole family

There is room to gather in the open living and dining areas, and three bedrooms when everyone needs a quieter moment. A fully equipped kitchen and washing machine make longer stays practical. The house welcomes a maximum of six guests, including children. A high chair is available and a cot can be requested. As the house is beside the water, children need supervision near the lake.

Discover Dalarna beyond the beach

Falun is approximately 25 minutes away by car, with town life and the historic Falu Mine. Bjursås Berg & Sjö is around 20 minutes away, while Leksand Sommarland is about 40 minutes away. Mix days by the water with local excursions, or simply spend more time at the house.

Arriving from Stockholm or Arlanda

Stockholm and Stockholm Arlanda Airport are approximately three hours away by car. These are approximate driving times; plan your route for your arrival time and allow for stops and traffic. A car makes it easier to explore the area from the house.

Questions before your stay

Is this a private house or shared accommodation?

You book the whole house for your group, with three bedrooms and a maximum of six guests.

How close is the beach?

The private sandy beach is approximately 25 metres from the house, directly on Lake Rogsjön.

Are the sauna, boat and SUP available?

The house has a private sauna and relaxation area. A boat and SUP are available; guests pay for the petrol used by the motorboat.

Can first-time guests request a direct booking?

Yes. Choose your dates and guests, then send a booking request. Your host reviews the request personally; your stay is confirmed only after you receive a booking confirmation.
