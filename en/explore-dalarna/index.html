OUR PERSONAL GUIDE

The best thing about Dalarna?
You don't have to choose just one thing.

Our own favourites and the tips we share with our guests. Alternate days of discovery with lazy days by the lake – and let your mood decide.

01 / Close to nature

Little adventures. Deep breaths.

Forest trails, lakes and views. Pack a picnic and take your time.

Close to the house

Rogsjön & Grycksbo

Törnbergs stig

Walking · MTB

A forest trail with gentle bends and local history. As noted in our guidebook, it emerges roughly 500 metres from the house. Törnbergs väg itself is about 3.3 km; getting there and returning adds to the distance.

View the trail map↗
Alexander's favourite

Bjursås

Ärtknubben

Views · Picnic stop

One of our personal favourites, in the hills across the lake. Bring a flask of coffee and enjoy the forest and views. Check the access road and trail before setting off.

Plan your walk↗
A local gem

Between Rogsjön & Utgrycken

Lurån

Walking · Local history

Follow the water through the forest past traces of old mills and water power. The described circular walk is about 2.7 km. Some sections are rocky, and high water can affect crossings.

Explore the walk↗

04 / ON TWO WHEELS

A ride from the house.
Through forests and villages.

Rogsjön, Bergsgården and Grycksbo, with Törnbergs stig along the way. A ride for those who want to explore the area by mountain bike.

approx. 32kilometres
approx. 520metres of ascent
2.5–4hours, approximately
Before you ride

About 10% of the route is very hilly. This map is an overview. To find your way, also use a navigation app such as Strava with the correct route loaded. Ask us for the route before setting off.

Estimates are from our route map. Time varies with your pace, stops and the terrain.

Bjursås Berg & Sjö is our recommendation for downhill skiing. Plan around open slopes and the weather. Afterwards, enjoy the warmth of the fire or a relaxing sauna evening by Rogsjön.
