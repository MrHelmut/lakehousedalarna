WELCOME

Designed for lake views, space and quiet days

On a headland, only 25 metres from a private sandy beach, this family holiday home combines its original 1960s character with a new extension completed in 2026. Around 200 m², panoramic windows and a 5.7-metre ceiling create space for up to six guests.

It is a peaceful place for swimming, nature, sauna evenings and long dinners on the terrace, while still being only about 25 minutes from Falun.

GUEST ACCESS

What guests can use

Guests have access to the whole house, outdoor spaces, sauna with relaxation area, sandy beach and one jetty. The plot is about 2,800 m² and also has a swing and a small playhouse for children.

Please note that the hot tub, guest house and other outbuildings are not included in the rental and are for private use.

Questions before your stay

Who is the house suitable for?

The whole house accommodates up to six guests and suits families, couples and friends. Lake House Dalarna, also known as Solsidan Dalarna, is by Lake Rogsjön in Dalarna, Sweden, about 25 minutes from Falun.

Are the sauna and boat included?

The sauna and a boat with an outboard motor are included. Life jackets and some fishing equipment are available. Guests pay for the petrol they use and refill the fuel can before departure.

Practical information for your boat trip

Guests pay for the petrol they use and refill the fuel can before departure. If you cannot refill it in time, please let us know in advance so we can try to arrange a solution.

Open the map on your phone to see marked rocks and shallows. Use it as a reference during your boat trip, keep a lookout and travel slowly near the shore. Not all obstacles may be marked.

Open the lake map

Are cleaning and bed linen included in the total price?

Mandatory final cleaning by our cleaning company costs 850 SEK per stay and bed linen costs 150 SEK per guest. Both are included in the total price shown when you select dates and guests.

Can I charge an electric car?

Charging costs 3 SEK per kWh. Connect your car as usual; charging starts automatically according to the house charging schedule.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are not allowed.

How do I book directly?

Choose dates and the number of guests on our booking page to see an estimated total price, then send a request. Your booking is only confirmed after personal confirmation from us. Airbnb is also available as an alternative.

OUR STORY

A house with a history

1960s

Alexander’s grandparents, Gunvor and Olle, bought the plot in the 1960s to build a holiday home. First, they built a 10-square-metre cabin from reclaimed timber. They lived there with their daughter Ulla, Alexander’s mother, while building the 30-square-metre log house. The little cabin is now a guest cottage.

Olle built the jetty by hand, without machinery, and shaped the beach in front of the house. The family estimates that he moved several hundred tonnes of stone. Alexander and Malin later continued renovating the jetty.

2006

The family enjoyed the house from the 1960s onwards. In August 2006, Olle visited for the last time before his death in December that year.

2011

In 2011, Alexander began restoring the property. Since then, he has devoted all available time to refining and improving this family place.
