Plan your journey to Lugnet

The Nordic World Ski Championships take place at Lugnet in Falun from 24 February to 7 March 2027. The house is outside town and suits guests travelling by car. Allow extra time for winter roads, event traffic and getting from the car park to the arena.

Check the organiser's current information on tickets, parking and transport before travelling. Arena tickets and transport are not included in your stay.

Questions about your World Championships stay

How far is the house from Lugnet?

Approximately 25 minutes by car in normal conditions. The house is beside Lake Rogsjön outside Falun. Allow extra time for winter roads, event traffic and parking.

How many guests can stay?

A maximum of six guests, including children. You rent the whole house with three bedrooms and a shared kitchen, living room and dining area.

What does accommodation cost during the championships?

Select your dates and number of guests in the calendar for an estimated price, including cleaning and bed linen. The final price is confirmed personally. The website's usual direct booking discount does not apply during the World Championships period.

Can first-time guests book directly?

Yes. First-time and returning guests are welcome to send a booking request. Alexander replies personally. Sending a request does not instantly confirm a booking.

Are World Championships tickets included?

No. You book your accommodation with us and buy arena tickets separately through the organiser. Solsidan Dalarna is an independent holiday house, not an official World Championships package.
